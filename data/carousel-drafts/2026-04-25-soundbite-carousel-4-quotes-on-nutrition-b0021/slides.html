RDAs

The RDA is a floor, not a ceiling.

RDAs were built to keep sailors from getting scurvy. They are the minimum you need to avoid deficiency. Optimum is a different question, and it is the question most patients should be asking.

WHAT I TELL PATIENTS ABOUT NUTRITION

“RDAs are based on maritime law — they're the minimum you need to NOT get scurvy, not the optimum to thrive.”
