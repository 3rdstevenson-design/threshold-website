HOW IT WORKS

Four things that make stretching actually work.

◈
Low intensity
Staying at a 2 to 3 out of 10 keeps the nervous system calm enough to let the tissue lengthen.
◉
Two-minute holds
Duration under two minutes rarely produces lasting range of motion change in a single bout.
◆
Correct muscle targeted
Stretching the wrong structure near the right joint changes nothing, so identification matters.
◇
Consistent application
One session shows you it works, repeated sessions build the change that sticks over time.

THE DETAILS

The dose that actually drives change.

Two to three out of ten intensity. Two to three minutes per muscle. That's the window where the nervous system stops guarding and the tissue responds. Most people never touch this range because it feels too easy.

2-3/10 effort2-3 min per stretchTarget one muscle at a timeRepeat across sessions

Apply the right dose and you'll feel a difference inside the same session.

06
THE PATH

How to start stretching with precision.

01
Identify the limiting muscle
Find the specific structure that's restricting your movement, not just the area that feels tight.
02
Set your intensity at 2-3/10
Move into the stretch until you feel mild tension, then stop well before any sensation of pain.
03
Hold for a full two minutes
Use a timer and stay in position, letting the tension soften naturally rather than forcing it.
04
Test movement immediately after
Check the range of motion you were chasing right away so you can see the direct effect.
