## Draft B — Hot take Reel
**Hook:** You bought the sleep mask, the glucose monitor, and seven supplements, and you still aren't eating enough protein.

**Spoken script (word for word):**
Okay, hot take. Most of you are optimizing the wrong thing first. You've got the sleep tracking, the fasting window, the gut protocol, the supplement stack that needs its own spreadsheet, and you still can't hit your protein. I see it all the time.

Here's a ranking idea I got from Mike T. Nelson. You score every habit two ways. How big the physiological effect is, times how likely you are to actually do it. Rank everything by that, and protein sits at number one. Sleep, for the record, lands around number eight. Sleep still matters a ton. It's just slow to move and hard to change, so it's a rough place to start.

Protein is the opposite. It's a huge effect and it's easy to do. And the research backs the dose. To max out muscle protein synthesis you want about 0.4 grams per kilo per meal, across at least four meals, which lands you around 1.6 grams per kilo for the day.

So here's my call. Before you buy another gadget, get 40 grams of protein at four meals and hold it for two weeks. That one move beats the shiny protocol you'll quit by next Thursday.

Be Good.

Learn Lots.
