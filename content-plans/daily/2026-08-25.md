## Draft B — Hot take Reel
**Hook:** Ice bath or hot bath after a football match, and 3 days later, there was no difference between them.

**Spoken script (word for word):**

22 guys played a simulated football match, and then got either cold water immersion or hot water immersion, straight after, again at 24 hours, and again at 48.

The match did what a match does. Voluntary contraction dropped 7%, sprint power dropped almost 9% at the 24 hour mark, and soreness climbed for 2 days.

It's 22 guys and a simulated match, so hold it loosely. I'd still take that trade.

The boring stuff is still what moves the needle. Sleep, food, and an actual plan for the 48 hours after a game.
